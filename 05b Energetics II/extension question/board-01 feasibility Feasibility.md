# Feasibility

1. What does feasible mean in terms of ΔG?
Answer: Feasible means ΔG < 0.

2. What does favourable mean in terms of ΔH?
Answer: Favourable means ΔH < 0.

3. Why is a reaction with ΔH < 0 and ΔS > 0 feasible at all temperatures?
Answer: In ΔG = ΔH - TΔS, ΔH is negative and -TΔS is also negative, so ΔG remains negative.
